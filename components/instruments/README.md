# Instrument component boundary

The analytical production instruments remain in `components/solar-os` until
their behavior-preserving extraction milestone. Milestone 4 integrates
Observatory Instruments 01–07 one increment at a time without moving or
duplicating those HTML workspaces.

Instruments 01–05 are mounted spatially under `components/3d/instruments`.
Instrument 01 is a configured-orientation logical system schematic. Instrument
02 is a static, texture-free one-hour atmospheric-transfer schematic driven by
the bounded `AtmosphericObservationFrameV1`; it does not encode cloud altitude,
type, geometry or motion, wind direction, sub-hour causality or measured/live
plant response. Instrument 03 retains all 8,760 exact unrounded canonical
hourly AC samples in one `InstancedBufferGeometry` draw path. Chronology controls
angle, a display-only annual-peak ratio controls radius, meteorological season
controls muted colour and quality only reduces luminance. It owns no frame loop,
timer, random source or HTML chart. Instruments 01 and 02 persist at reduced
emphasis when Instrument 03 forms.

Instrument 04 is a normalized Array Engineering orientation rig driven by the
fail-closed `ArrayEngineeringFrameV1`. It shows one explicitly nonphysical
collection aperture, its resolved normal, the authoritative linked Sun vector
and the source angle-of-incidence relationship. It does not render module
count, rows, capacity as physical area, named equipment, terrain, surveyed
horizon or moving shadows. Instruments 01–03 persist at reduced emphasis when
Instrument 04 forms.

Instrument 05 is a reach-triggered Scenario Comparison formation driven by the
fail-closed `ScenarioComparisonFrameV1`. Five normalized, nonphysical spatial
chambers compare one configured baseline with four canonical deterministic
stress cases; response-column height is a signed annual-generation comparison
scale, not physical height, probability or exact numerical authority. The
semantic HTML table retains exact annual generation, screened value, local
supply and deltas, together with provenance and all ten unavailable-layer
disclosures. Instruments 01–04 persist at reduced emphasis when Instrument 05
forms.

Authoritative numerical facts, lifecycle, quality, provenance, limitations and
interaction remain semantic HTML. Instrument 01 opens `DT`; Instrument 02
opens `WA`; both retain the exact linked index. Instrument 03 opens `AF` and
retains the annual empirical `n = 4` spread and unsupported-layer disclosures.
Instrument 04 opens `AE` and exposes the existing accessible screening controls
without resetting a restored device-local branch. Instrument 05 opens `SR`
through the same typed activation request and retains the exact linked index.
Positions 01–05 are mounted, positions 06–07 remain placeholders and positions
08–14 remain reserved. All five instruments share the one persistent
frame/canvas; Instrument 05 stays outside the initial spatial closure and is
loaded only when reached.
